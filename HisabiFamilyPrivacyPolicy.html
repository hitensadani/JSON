Hisabi Family: privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026. Applies to the Hisabi Family Android app (package com.hitsofttech.hisabi.family) by HitSoftTech, version 3.3.1-hf.27 and later.

Hisabi Family keeps your accounts, entries and "My data" logs on your phone. We (HitSoftTech) do not run our own servers, do not show ads, do not use analytics, and do not sell or share your data. The app uses the internet only for the features listed below, and you can turn the internet off completely in Settings › Privacy › Use the internet.

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • Your books (accounts, entries, budgets), My data logs and their history, reminders and tasks.
  • AI chats, if you keep them. They are stored encrypted in the app's private storage.
  • Daily automatic backups of your book, in the app's folder on your phone (shown in the app). You can copy them elsewhere yourself.
  • An app log is written only if you turn on developer mode.

None of this is sent anywhere unless you use one of the features below. Android's own cloud backup of the app is turned off.

3. Permissions

  • Internet, network state: the features in section 2.
  • Location, Bluetooth, nearby Wi-Fi devices, local network: only to find family phones nearby for syncing without internet. Your location is not collected or sent.
  • Microphone: only for voice input and live talk with the AI, when you tap the mic.
  • Notifications, start at boot, wake lock: reminders, bill alerts and scheduled tasks.
  • Install packages (not in the Play version): to install updates downloaded from GitHub.

4. How long data is kept

  • Cloud (Firebase): a change is deleted as soon as the receiving phone has it; anything left over is deleted within 30 days. The family's record and key backup stay while the family uses internet sync and are deleted with "Delete family from the cloud". Your Google sign-in record stays until you delete your cloud data.
  • Crash reports: kept by Google for 90 days.
  • Your phone: until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Settings let you limit how long history, backups and AI chats are kept.

5. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn the internet, crash reports, AI, update checks and scheduled AI tasks on or off in Settings › Privacy.
  • Delete your cloud data in the app (Settings › Delete my cloud data), or a whole family (Family › Delete family from the cloud), or ask us: how to delete your data.
  • Delete AI chats, history and backups in the app at any time.

6. Security

Family data is encrypted on your phone before it is sent (AES-256-GCM, with keys protected by the Android Keystore), and signed so other phones can check who sent it. The server only sees encrypted data and the account ids it needs to route it. No method is perfectly secure; keep your phone locked and your recovery text safe.

7. Children

Hisabi Family is meant for adults managing household money. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Open-source software

Hisabi Family includes open-source software from GnuCash Pocket (Apache License 2.0). It is not made or endorsed by the GnuCash project.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data use changes, and change the date at the top. Earlier versions of the app (before 3.3.1-hf.27) kept cloud sync data until the family admin removed it and did not encrypt member names.
