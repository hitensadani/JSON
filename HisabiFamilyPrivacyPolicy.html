Hisabi Family: privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026. Applies to the Hisabi Family Android app (package com.hitsofttech.hisabi.family) by HitSoftTech, version 3.3.1-hf.27 and later.

Hisabi Family keeps your accounts, entries and "My data" logs on your phone. We (HitSoftTech) do not run our own servers, do not show ads, do not use analytics, and do not sell your data. The app uses the internet only for the features listed below, and you can turn the internet off completely in Settings › Privacy › Use the internet (nearby and file sync still work).

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • Your books (accounts, entries, budgets), My data logs and their history, reminders and tasks.
  • AI chats, if you keep them, stored encrypted in the app's private storage. Chats not used for 90 days move to an archive on the phone that is opened only when you ask.
  • Daily automatic backups of your book, in the app's folder on your phone (shown on Home and in Settings › Data on this phone). You can lock them with a passphrase and copy them elsewhere yourself.
  • An app log is written only if you turn on developer mode; otherwise nothing is logged.
  • Your own Gemini API key, if you add one, stored encrypted with the Android Keystore.

None of this is sent anywhere unless you use one of the features below. Android's own cloud backup of the app is turned off.

3. Permissions

  • Internet, network state: the features in section 2.
  • Location, Bluetooth, nearby Wi-Fi devices: only to find family phones nearby for syncing without internet (Android requires the location permission for this). Your location is not read, collected or sent.
  • Microphone: only for voice input and live talk with the AI, when you tap the mic.
  • Notifications, start at boot, wake lock: reminders, bill alerts and scheduled tasks.
  • Install packages (not in the Play version): to install updates downloaded from GitHub.

4. How long data is kept

  • Cloud (Firebase): a change, request or reply is deleted by the phones as soon as the receiving phone has it. Items that could not be delivered carry an expiry time (1 to 30 days; a phone's "read up to" mark 60 days), and the family's phones delete them at their next sync once it has passed. The family's record and its encrypted key backup stay while the family uses internet sync (they expire after a year without use) and are deleted with "Delete family from the cloud". If no phone of a family syncs any more, its last encrypted items stay until a phone syncs again or the family is deleted from the cloud. Your Google sign-in record stays until you delete your cloud data. You can see and delete your own items in Family › your family › Cloud data.
  • Crash reports: kept by Google for 90 days.
  • Your phone (until you delete it or uninstall the app), with these defaults, which you can change in Settings:
    • My data history (undo) and deleted rows: 1 year (or Forever, or 90 days).
    • AI chats: moved to the archive after 90 days (or 30 or 180) and kept Forever (or 1 year, or 90 days).
    • Automatic backups: the last 10, plus one per month for 12 months; older ones are deleted.
    • App log: none, unless developer mode is on (then the last 3,000 lines).

5. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn the internet, internet sync, crash reports, the update check and scheduled AI tasks on or off in Settings › Privacy. AI is never used until you agree; "Reset privacy consent" in Settings › AI asks again.
  • Delete your cloud data in the app (Settings › Delete my cloud data), single items (Family › your family › Cloud data), or a whole family (Family › Delete family from the cloud), or ask us: how to delete your data.
  • Delete AI chats, history and backups in the app at any time, or everything at once: Settings › Data on this phone › Delete everything on this phone.

6. Security

Family data is encrypted on your phone before it is sent (AES-256-GCM, with keys protected by the Android Keystore and each family's keys kept apart), and signed so other phones can check who sent it. The server only sees encrypted data plus the account ids, invite codes and times it needs to route it. A phone with an app version from before 3.3.1-hf.27 that joins a family sends its name and email unencrypted in its join request, which is deleted when the admin decides (at most 7 days). No method is perfectly secure; keep your phone locked and your recovery text safe.

7. Children

Hisabi Family is meant for adults managing household money. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data use changes, and change the date at the top. Earlier versions of the app (before 3.3.1-hf.27) kept cloud sync data until the family admin removed it and did not encrypt member names.
