Hisabi Family: delete your data

Hisabi Family by HitSoftTech keeps your books on your phone. The cloud (Google Firebase) only carries encrypted changes between family phones, and the phones delete each one as soon as the other phone has it. Anything that could not be delivered has an expiry time and is deleted by the family's phones at their next sync after it has passed.

Delete it in the app

  1. Your cloud data: Settings › Delete my cloud data. This deletes what your phone left in the cloud and your sign-in for the app, and asks the family's admin phone to remove you. Your books stay on your phone.
  2. Single items: Family › your family › Cloud data shows what you have in the cloud (kind, date and size, never the contents) and deletes one item or all of yours.
  3. A whole family (main admin phone): Family › Delete family from the cloud. Every document of the family is deleted from the cloud. The books stay on the phones.
  4. Only stop syncing: Settings › Sign out.
  5. Everything on the phone: Settings › Data on this phone › Delete everything on this phone (it offers a backup first), or uninstall the app.

Ask us to delete it

If you can't use the app any more (for example, the phone is lost), email hitsofttech@gmail.com from the Google account you used for family sync, with the subject "Hisabi Family data deletion". We delete your sign-in and your family's cloud documents within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is not

  • Deleted: your Firebase sign-in (Google account id and email), the encrypted changes and requests your phone sent, and how far your phone had read. For a family you run: everything of that family in the cloud.
  • Not in the cloud, so nothing to delete there: your books, entries, My data, backups and AI chats. They live only on your phone; to remove them, delete them in the app or uninstall it.
  • Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics) hold no names or amounts and are deleted by Google after 90 days.
  • What you sent to the AI is handled by Google under the Gemini API terms; we keep no copy of it.
